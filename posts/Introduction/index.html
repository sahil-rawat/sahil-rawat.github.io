Hello World! I'm Sahil - Software Developer & Security Enthusiast

Sahil Singh Rawat Jul 1, 2021 2021-07-01T16:24:00+05:30
Mar 18 2026-03-18T20:19:13+05:30 1 min

Hello everyone! I’m Sahil Singh Rawat, a Software Developer based in India. currently working at Morgan Stanley.

My professional work focuses on building and maintaining scalable software, but my curiosity has always led me toward the “offensive” side of technology. I’ve always believed that the best way to write truly secure code is to understand exactly how it can be broken.

Most of my security knowledge is self-taught through hands-on practice. I actively participate in various hackathons, CTFs and I am also an active player @hackthebox with rank Pro Hacker and @tryhackme with rank Guru

What is this blog about?

I created this space to document my learning experiences, technical challenges, and personal research. While my posts primarily revolve around cybersecurity, they aren’t strictly limited to it. Here’s a glimpse of what I’ll be sharing:

  • Bug Bounty: Insights and methodologies from my research.
  • Under the Hood: Deep dives into system internals and secure development.
  • General Tech: Anything else I find valuable or interesting in my spare time.

I hope you find these posts useful and perhaps learn something new along the way. I’m always looking to grow, so feedback is much appreciated!
